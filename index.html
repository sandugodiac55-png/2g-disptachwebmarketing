WHAT WE DO

Car hauling has its own rules

Its own brokers, its own paperwork, its own way of getting paid. A general freight dispatcher learns that on your truck. We already know it.

Load sourcing

Boards and direct broker contacts worked daily, filtered for your trailer size and the lanes you want to run. You see loads that pay for the miles, not everything that exists.

Rate negotiation

Every load is negotiated before it reaches you. You set a minimum rate per mile and nothing below it gets sent.

Broker setup

Carrier packets, agreements, certificates of insurance and W-9s go out from our side. New broker relationships get opened without you making a call.

Paperwork and invoicing

Rate confirmations, bills of lading, gate passes, invoices. COD and COP loads tracked so nothing gets delivered with money still owed.

Factoring support

We work with your factoring company so invoices go out clean the first time and the cash comes in without a second round of emails.

The difficult calls

Detention, damage claims, a broker going quiet on payment. You keep driving. We stay on the phone.

THE ARRANGEMENT

Nothing you have to sign your way out of

No contract

Week to week. If it is not working, you say so and we are done. No termination clause, no notice period.

No forced dispatch

Every load comes to you first. You say yes or no. Nothing gets booked behind your back.

Percentage of gross

We are paid out of what you earn, so a weak rate costs us too. The exact percentage depends on fleet size — ask on the call.

Your authority stays yours

Your MC, your insurance, your factoring, your bank account. We dispatch. We do not sit between you and your money.
